Welcome to the Video Quiz Study

Goal of the Challenge

In this study, you will watch short video clips and answer multiple-choice questions about what you see.

Rules

  • Watch each video carefully before answering.
  • Select the option that best describes what is happening.
  • Do not refresh the page once you begin.
  • You can only move forward (no going back).

Notes

The study will take about 30–45 minutes.

Study Consent

Informed Consent

You are invited to take part in a study about how people recognize motion. You will view about 50 short motion videos and choose which description fits best.

  • Time: 30–45 minutes.
  • Risks: Minimal, possible mild eye fatigue. You may stop at any time.
  • Confidentiality: Responses are anonymous. Signed consent forms will be stored securely for six months and then destroyed.
  • Voluntary Participation: You may withdraw at any time without penalty.
